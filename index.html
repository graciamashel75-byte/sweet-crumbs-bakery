About Us

SweetCrumbs Bakery was founded in 2010 by Grace & Samuel Kariuki with a single oven, a family recipe book, and a dream to bring authentic, artisan baking to the heart of Nairobi.

Over the years, we have grown into a beloved neighbourhood bakery trusted by thousands of families, offices, and event planners across the city. Our bakers rise before dawn every single day to ensure that what lands on your table is always fresh, always flavourful, and always made with care.

We believe baking is an art, not a factory process. That is why we never use artificial preservatives, and we source our flour, eggs, and dairy from certified local farms within 100 km of Nairobi.
